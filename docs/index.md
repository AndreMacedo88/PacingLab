# PacingLab

PacingLab is an Android app that turns the data of a Fitbit watch into training analysis for runners:
heart rate zones based on your own maximum, threshold and resting heart rate, reps and recoveries of
interval sessions, training load, fitness, fatigue and form, readiness from overnight HRV, resting heart
rate and sleep, and route maps.

It reads your data from Health Connect on the phone and, optionally, from the Google Health API. Your
data stays on your phone: PacingLab has no account, no server and no analytics.

- [Privacy policy](privacy.md)
